# Quick checklist

1. Ensure Cobaya (or equivalent) can load `planck_2018_lensing.native` or `.CMBMarged`.
2. Feed final locked \(C_L^{\phi\phi}\) and control \(C_L^{\phi\phi}\) into it.
3. Report \(\chi^2_{\rm locked}\), \(\chi^2_{\rm control}\), \(\Delta\chi^2\).
4. Confirm all final-reference numbers unchanged.

That is the full official lensing test.
